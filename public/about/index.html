Who we are

Prevention, transparency, and a direct line between practitioners and the people looking for them.

FindWell Directory was born out of a simple but urgent mission: to change the conversation around healthcare in our country. Too often, access to healing is filtered through layers of insurance systems, hidden costs, and a pharmaceutical-driven model that doesn't reflect the diverse ways people actually seek wellness. We believe health should be rooted in prevention, transparency, and the empowerment of individuals to choose what works best for their bodies and their lives.

This project grew out of our work with Welliva Health, a new approach to healthcare financing that focuses on integrative and lifestyle-based care. While Welliva develops an alternative model of coverage, FindWell serves as its natural partner: a place where practitioners and patients connect directly, without middlemen. Practitioners openly share their credentials, services and pricing, and the people using the directory help us see the real needs in communities across the country.

What we verify

For licensed professions we check the license number with the board that issued it and note the date. Where a discipline has no license, we record the training, the hours and any certification, and say plainly that no license exists. More about this

What we don't do

We don't evaluate clinical claims, host reviews, or vouch for outcomes. A listing means the credentials are as stated, nothing more. Complementary care works best alongside medical care, and we assume you have a physician too.
