About the directory

There is no centralised system for non-insurance-based care, which makes a first appointment a guess. We collect the same facts from every practitioner and publish them in the same shape, so the comparison is yours to make.

  • Every listing carries state licensure where it applies, years in practice, pricing structure, insurance status and direct contact details.
  • For disciplines with no licensure route — Ayurveda, herbalism, energy medicine — we publish training and voluntary certification instead, and say plainly that no license exists.
  • No middlemen, no commissions, no listing fees. Nothing routes through us and no one pays for placement.
