List your practice

Listing is free and always will be. We publish exactly what you send, and where we check a credential with the issuing board, your listing says so and names the date. It takes about ten minutes.

Listings are for individual practitioners. Each listing carries one person's license, training and years in practice, which is what lets us tell a patient how those credentials were established. A practice as a whole cannot hold that, so practitioners who work together are listed side by side rather than as one entry. Practising under a business name is perfectly fine.
