About the practice

Akiva Benav is a Functional Integrative Medicine Practitioner specializing in Nervous System Regulation. He helps clients with stress and anxiety, gut and digestive issues, sleep problems, and fatigue through telehealth-based programs in nervous system regulation, gut–brain work, and breathwork therapy.
