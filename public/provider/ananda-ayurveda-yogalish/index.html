About the practice

Ayurvedic consultations and education, including CEU provision for NCBTMB and Yoga Alliance.

A husband-and-wife practice. Consultations with Tanja run in two parts: a new-client intake of about two hours, then a report of findings of about an hour. Recommendations cover food as medicine, herbs, aromatherapy, lifestyle, yoga, meditation, sound and colour therapy, Ayurvedic treatments and cleanses. Bill, the panchakarma therapist, provides Ayurvedic treatments and holistic massage. The practice also runs workshops, trainings, lectures and classes, with CEUs for massage therapists, bodyworkers and yoga teachers.
