About the practice

Anastasia Dosov Nutrition & Wellness Consulting is an integrative and functional nutrition practice specializing in gastrointestinal health, gut microbiome restoration, metabolic health, and pediatric nutrition, with a particular focus on autism spectrum disorder (ASD) and ADHD. Our services include comprehensive nutritional assessments, advanced functional laboratory testing, and personalized dietary and nutraceutical interventions tailored to each individual's needs. We combine evidence-informed clinical nutrition with a whole-person approach to address contributing factors, support physiological function, and promote long-term health and well-being.

Anastasia Dosov Nutrition & Wellness Consulting is a functional nutrition practice specializing in gastrointestinal health, gut microbiome restoration, metabolic health, and pediatric nutrition, with a particular focus on autism spectrum disorder (ASD) and other neurodevelopmental conditions. Founded by Anastasia Dosov, MS, CNS, LDN, the practice combines evidence-based clinical nutrition, functional medicine principles, and advanced laboratory testing to develop personalized nutritional interventions that address underlying physiological imbalances. Areas of expertise include the gut–brain axis, digestive disorders, intestinal dysbiosis, mitochondrial function, nutritional deficiencies, and dietary and nutraceutical interventions for children with ASD and ADHD. Anastasia is actively involved in clinical research exploring nutritional interventions in autism and pursues ongoing advanced education in microbiome science and functional medicine. The practice provides comprehensive nutritional assessments, individualized dietary and supplementation protocols, functional laboratory testing, and ongoing support for children and adults. Consultations are available virtually, with a focus on collaborative, and scientifically informed care.
