About the practice

Creating and planning end-of-life care for patients and families, from practical work such as advance care planning through to legacy projects and emotional and spiritual support.

Non-medical support with gentle, intuitive guidance. I meet people where they are and offer presence — talking through fears, exploring what gives peace, creating rituals that feel meaningful, and holding space for grief as it unfolds. No public premises; visits are by arrangement, in person or remotely.
