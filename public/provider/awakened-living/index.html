About the practice

Dr. Nibodhi has spent over 30 years giving private consultations to more than 30,000 people. As a Traditional Naturopath and Ayurvedic Practitioner, he supports people in transforming their health and consciousness. Drawing on Ayurveda, Vedic sciences, Yoga, Compassionate Inquiry, Jyotish and traditional naturopathy, he supports physical, emotional, and spiritual well-being. His approach helps cultivate balance, clarity, and resilience in body, mind, and Self. Each session is personalized. Dr. Nibodhi meets people with respect and attentiveness, creating a supportive space for meaningful healing and growth. His guidance is thoughtful, practical, and grounded in decades of experience.

Personal guidance, built around you. Every appointment starts with deep listening. Dr. Nibodhi takes time to understand the full picture of your needs. He focuses on identifying the root cause and creates a protocol tailored to your constitution and individual needs. Ayurvedic - A full evaluation of your constitution, current state of balance, and the root causes driving your symptoms. Vedic medical astrology - Your birth chart read through the lens of health. Identifying innate tendencies and what your body & mind need right now. Physical diagnostics - Tongue, nail, and eye analysis to assess organ function, digestive strength, and systemic imbalances. Emotional mapping -A compassionate look at how stress, emotional and mental patterns are affecting your physical health. Personalized protocol - Specific dietary guidelines, herbs, breathwork, meditation, and daily routines designed for your constitution. A detailed report. Your protocol, insights, and next steps.
