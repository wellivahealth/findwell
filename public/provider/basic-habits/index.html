About the practice

Isbel Cano is a Registered Dietitian and the founder of Basic Habits, dedicated to helping individuals and families build healthier lifestyles through personalized nutrition. Specializing in diabetes management, insulin resistance, and PCOS, Isbel provides practical dietary guidance tailored to individual needs. Her services also include pediatric nutrition and bilingual nutrition counseling, making expert guidance more accessible. Through evidence-based strategies and sustainable eating habits, Basic Habits empowers clients to improve their overall well-being and develop healthier relationships with food.

Isbel Cano is the founder of Basic Habits, dedicated to helping individuals and families achieve better health through personalized nutrition counseling. Specializing in diabetes management, insulin resistance, and PCOS, Isbel provides practical, evidence-based dietary guidance tailored to each client's unique needs and goals. Basic Habits also offers pediatric nutrition services to support children's healthy growth and development. Her approach focuses on building sustainable eating habits, improving overall well-being, and developing a healthier relationship with food. She empowers clients to make confident, lasting lifestyle changes.
