About the practice

I offer shamanic healing sessions and advanced life coaching for people navigating trauma, burnout, major transitions, and periods of uncertainty. With more than 40 years of international experience in trauma, resilience, leadership, and transformation, I blend powerful shamanic practices with research-grounded approaches to help clients release emotional and energetic blocks, reconnect with their deeper self and purpose, and move forward with greater clarity and grounded, sustainable change. I work with clients in person in Takoma Park, Maryland, serving DC, Maryland, and Virginia, as well as online worldwide

Credentials—

TrainingI hold a pluridisciplinary PhD from France, am a former University researcher and professor at the intersection of political anthropology, peace building, trauma healing and resilience, neuroscience and spirituality. For more than four decades, I have supported individuals and communities through trauma, uncertainty, and profound change—across nearly 90 countries, including some 30 affected by war and violent unrest. I was born a shaman, but it took a profound burnout for me to fully reconnect with my gifts and bring them into my work. I did an apprenticeship with a shaman in the US and was initiated in different cultures. My work brings together shamanic wisdom with extensive experience in trauma and resilience, neuropsychology, leadership, and peacebuilding. I work at the place where deep inner healing meets real life—helping people release what still holds them back, reconnect with their deeper self, and translate that clarity into the way they live, work, relate, and contribute to the world. My approach is direct, compassionate, and deeply grounded, with one goal: transformation that is not only profound, but integrated and sustainable.
