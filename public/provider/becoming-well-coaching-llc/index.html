Becoming Well Coaching provides virtual, whole-person wellness coaching for individuals who want to create positive, sustainable changes in their lives. Through a client-led approach, I help you look at the different areas of wellness—including physical, emotional, social, intellectual, spiritual, environmental, occupational, and financial well-being—and identify where you would like to grow. My approach is warm, supportive, and practical, with an emphasis on self-reflection, mindfulness, emotional awareness, and meaningful change. Rather than telling you what you should do, I help you explore what matters to you and develop greater awareness of the thoughts, habits, and patterns that may be affecting your overall well-being. All coaching sessions are offered virtually, making it convenient to receive support from wherever you are. I also offer a complimentary 15-minute consultation to discuss your goals, learn more about coaching, and determine whether it may be a good fit for you.
