About the practice

Catalina Acupuncture provides solutions for pain and illness, blending traditional Chinese medicine with modern, results-driven techniques for chronic pain, injuries, stress and a range of health conditions.

Nathan Anderson, L.Ac., founder of Catalina Acupuncture, takes time to listen and creates personalised treatment plans that go beyond relieving symptoms to target the root cause. Care is delivered through acupuncture, cupping, herbal therapy and lifestyle guidance, with the goal of restoring balance in body, mind and spirit.
