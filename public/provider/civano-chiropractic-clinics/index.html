Civano Chiropractic Clinics

Clint Mausser

Chiropractors

Credentials confirmed: 8583 with the Arizona Board of Chiropractic Examiners, 25 Aug 2026.

We're a small, close-knit chiropractic practice built around one-on-one care. Rather than a high-volume, in-and-out model, we take the time to really listen to our patients and understand what's going on before we treat it. Our approach is hands-on and personalized, focused on helping people find real relief from common issues like headaches, sciatica, low back pain, and indigestion — all within the scope of chiropractic care. We pride ourselves on being approachable and attentive, so patients feel heard, not rushed.

Ours is a small, intimate office — designed around true one-on-one care. We currently offer chiropractic adjustments along with stretching to support treatment, without the distraction of a large facility or a revolving door of patients. We're a growing practice, and while we don't yet offer additional modalities, we plan to expand our services in the coming months to years as the practice grows.
