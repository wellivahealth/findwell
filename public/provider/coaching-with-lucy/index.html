About the practice

Lucy Seligman is a certified coach and hypnotherapist who works with people who feel overwhelmed, stuck, anxious, or uncertain about what comes next. Using coaching, hypnotherapy, mindfulness, and other mind-body tools, she helps clients quiet the noise, uncover what is getting in the way, and find greater clarity, confidence, and ease as they move forward.
