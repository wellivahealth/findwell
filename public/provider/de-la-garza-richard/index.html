About the practice

Richard De La Garza is a licensed clinical social worker with extensive experience supporting veterans, men, and underserved communities. His work focuses on trauma therapy, PTSD treatment, depression, and anger management, with a strong emphasis on providing effective therapy for veterans. Drawing from his military background and leadership training, he offers a structured yet compassionate approach to care. His integrative methods combine evidence-based techniques to help clients build clarity, resilience, and long-term emotional strength.

Dr. Richard De La Garza is a Mexican American–Chicano Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW) dedicated to providing trauma-informed, action-driven mental health support. His practice focuses on helping adults, veterans, and military-connected individuals navigate trauma, anxiety, depression, PTSD, and major life transitions through a compassionate, collaborative, and practical approach. Specializing in trauma therapy in San Diego, Richard supports individuals from diverse backgrounds, including BIPOC, LGBTQ, and neurodivergent communities. Drawing on more than 20 years of professional experience, military service, and clinical expertise, he combines evidence-based therapeutic approaches with nervous system education, narrative therapy, and EMDR training. His approach emphasizes three key stages: Stabilize, Align, and Lead, helping clients build emotional stability, reconnect with their values, strengthen self-trust, and develop practical strategies for everyday challenges. Through personalized therapy and meaningful collaboration, Richard empowers individuals to move beyond survival mode and create more balanced, confident, and fulfilling lives.
