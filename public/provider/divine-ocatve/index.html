About the practice

Divine Octave | Holistic Wellness, Energy Healing & Spiritual Guidance

Divine Octave is a welcoming space for restoration, self-discovery, and deeper connection to the wisdom within.

Founded by Heidi, Divine Octave offers a holistic approach to mind, body, and spirit, weaving together Reiki energy healing, sound healing, Galactic Astrology, Akashic Records, and intuitive spiritual guidance.

With over 15 years of study across energy healing, herbal wisdom, yoga, astrology, and spiritual practices, Heidi brings a compassionate, intuitive, and grounded presence to each session.

Whether you're navigating a life transition, feeling overwhelmed, seeking spiritual clarity, or simply longing to reconnect with yourself, each experience offers an opportunity to slow down, explore your inner landscape, and gain a deeper understanding of your unique journey.

Specializing in Galactic Astrology and Galactic Akashic Records, Divine Octave offers opportunities to explore the soul's journey through symbolic, spiritual, and cosmic perspectives. These sessions encourage personal reflection, self-awareness, and a deeper connection to your own inner wisdom.

Online and distance sessions are available nationwide, allowing you to experience personalized spiritual guidance and select energy healing offerings from wherever you feel most comfortable.

At Divine Octave, the intention is not to become someone new, but to gently return to the wisdom already within you.
