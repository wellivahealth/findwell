About the practice

Emily provides personalized, functional and holistic nutrition counseling for women with PCOS/PMOS who want to better understand their symptoms and feel more confident managing their health. Through her 1:1 Hormone Harmony Method, she takes a root-cause approach that considers nutrition, lifestyle, stress, sleep, and mindset to support hormonal health and sustainable changes without restrictive diets or one-size-fits-all plans. Her virtual practice offers compassionate, individualized guidance, education, and accountability to help women work toward more regular menstrual cycles, clearer skin, and greater confidence in their health.

Emily specializes in virtual nutrition counseling for women with PCOS/PMOS who are ready to take a more personalized approach to their health. Her practice combines functional and holistic nutrition with individualized lifestyle support to help women better understand their symptoms and identify factors that may be contributing to their hormonal concerns.

Through her signature 1:1 Hormone Harmony Method, Emily helps women build a stronger foundation for hormonal health by addressing nutrition, stress, sleep, digestion, movement, and mindset. Rather than relying on restrictive diets or quick fixes, she emphasizes sustainable, realistic changes tailored to each woman's unique needs, preferences, and goals. Emily believes women deserve to feel heard, supported, and empowered to make informed decisions about their health. Her compassionate, collaborative approach provides the education, guidance, and accountability women need to work toward more regular cycles, clearer skin, and a greater sense of confidence and control over their health.
