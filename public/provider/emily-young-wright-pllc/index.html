About the practice

I provide virtual counseling to adults experiencing anxiety, depression, grief, trauma, chronic illness, chronic pain, relationship challenges, and major life transitions. I have particular experience supporting people whose health, functioning, and sense of identity have changed.

My professional background includes neurorehabilitation, community health, intensive outpatient treatment, and private practice. I have experience supporting adults navigating chronic health conditions, pain, disability, medical stress, changes in functioning, and identity transitions, as well as anxiety, depression, grief, and trauma.

TrainingPrior to becoming a therapist, I worked for a decade as a Certified Therapeutic Recreation Specialist (CTRS), supporting adults navigating neurological injuries, chronic health conditions, disability, and changes in functioning and independence. My clinical experience includes working with adults experiencing anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, chronic pain, medical stress, relationship challenges, and major life transitions. My therapeutic approach is relational and collaborative, drawing from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), attachment-informed perspectives, and parts-work approaches. I value creating a supportive, nonjudgmental space where clients can better understand themselves, explore patterns, and develop new ways of responding to life's challenges.
