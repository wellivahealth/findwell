About the practice

Evidence-based, physiology-first nutrition support, individualized to your goals, preferences, and lifestyle. I'll work with you to uncover what's driving your health concerns and collaboratively guide you towards a better quality of life. Clients come to me for support with body composition, sports performance, gut health, endocrine disorders, and health concerns that haven't responded to generic advice, as well as anyone simply looking to support their healthspan and longevity, or reach their fullest health potential.

Emma Harvie Nutrition is a virtual nutrition practice serving clients nationwide. Emma Harvie, MS, CNS, LN, is a Licensed Nutritionist specializing in functional medicine, longevity, and sports performance nutrition. Her approach is physiology-first and root-cause focused. Rather than handing out generic meal plans or restrictive diets, she builds individualized strategies around each client’s goals, lab data, and lifestyle. Clients range from athletes chasing performance and recovery to people working to improve energy, body composition, metabolic health, and long-term well-being. Every program includes a personalized nutrition plan and a written protocol after each session, so clients know exactly what to do between visits. The goal is sustainable change built to fit real life, not short-term fixes.
