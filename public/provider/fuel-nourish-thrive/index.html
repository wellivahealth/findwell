About the practice

I am a licensed dietitian nutritionist with a background in functional and integrative health and nutrition. I've had the privilege of training under and learning from some of the foremost leaders in the field of functional and integrative health, including, Kara Fitzgerald, Aviva Romm, and staff at Mark Hyman's Ultra Wellness Center – all trailblazers and visionaries. I also have a certification in canine nutrition

My interest in food policy, integrative nutrition, and public health has guided me throughout my educational and professional life. Even when I was very young, healthful and intuitive eating and living has been a passion and now I am lucky enough to pursue it as a career. In addition to clinical work, I love researching and writing and am published in Forbes, MindBodyGreen, Yahoo, Real Simple, and CleanPlates. I believe in real food and real ingredients and I am always busy in the kitchen creating healthful, tasty, and nutrient-dense recipes. I use a practical, positive approach to help individuals improve their overall well being, achieve their goals, and develop sustainable habits to lead a healthy and happy life. I treasure the opportunity to be part of one’s journey and find the foods that nourish both body and soul.
