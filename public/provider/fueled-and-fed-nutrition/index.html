About the practice

Alisha offers 1:1 nutrition counseling, family nutrition counseling, workshops and webinars. She specializes in GI health, nutrition for mental health, and sports nutrition. She is based in Alexandria VA and practices remotely across DC, VA and MD.

The main force behind Fueled and Fed Nutrition is a deep desire to help you fuel your long-term performance, nutrition, and lifestyle goals through sustainable changes to your diet. We believe in client-driven care and will partner with you to determine a comprehensive plan based on your goals, lifestyle, and health status. Whether you’re looking for nutritious recipes or you need professional guidance in order to address a specific health issue, our nutritionists are ready to help you reach your dietary goals.

Fees

Initial visit $225, follow up visits $80-115. In-network with Aetna, BCBS, and Cigna. Sliding scale available. Please inquire for workshop and webinar prices.
