About the practice

Shamika T. Brown is a Licensed Massage Therapist specializing in advanced somatic recovery and lymphatic health. As the founder of Healing Through Energy LLC, she operates a premier Recovery Lab in the Antelope Valley, where clinical precision meets a high-end, concierge experience. With a deep focus on Somatic Hydrotherapy and Trauma-Informed bodywork, Shamika helps clients navigate the intersection of physical tension and nervous system regulation. Her practice is known for its VIP Men’s Wellness protocols—offering discreet, performance-based recovery services—and her signature Somatic Date Night rituals designed for couples seeking deeper connection. Utilizing state-of-the-art hydro-somatic techniques, Shamika provides a "Total Physical Protection" environment where luxury and healing are indistinguishable.
