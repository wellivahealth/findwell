About the practice

Love & Light Palms is a holistic wellness practice based in Columbus, Ohio, offering Usui Reiki Healing, Chakra Balancing, Aura Cleansing, and Akashic Records Readings. Services focus on relaxation, energetic balance, personal reflection, and overall well-being. In-person Reiki experiences are available in Columbus, with distance Reiki offered to clients regardless of location. Each experience is designed to provide a peaceful, compassionate, and welcoming environment.

Welcome to Love & Light Palms, where Healing Begins Within. Founded by Charla Woods-Favors, a Usui Reiki Level II practitioner, Love & Light Palms provides compassionate, personalized holistic wellness experiences in Columbus, Ohio, and remotely. Services include Reiki Healing, Chakra Balancing, Aura Cleansing, and Akashic Records Readings. Our approach encourages relaxation, energetic balance, self-awareness, and personal growth in a peaceful, judgment-free environment. We offer a complimentary 20-minute first-time experience by appointment, allowing newcomers to discover Reiki in a welcoming and comfortable setting. Whether you're seeking a moment of stillness or exploring a deeper connection with yourself, you are welcome here. You Matter. You Belong Here. Visit www.loveandlightpalms.com to explore our services and schedule an experience.
