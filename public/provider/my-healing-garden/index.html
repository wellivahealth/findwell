About the practice

At My Healing Garden, I offer personalized distance energy healing and remote Quantum Biofeedback sessions, along with Energy Scans & Chakra Clearing and Intuitive Readings. I also offer private healing retreats at My Healing Sanctuary in the mountains of North Idaho.

My Healing Garden is a holistic energy healing practice based in the mountains of North Idaho, offering distance and remote sessions to clients wherever they are. I offer Distance Energy Healing, remote Quantum Biofeedback, Energy Scans & Chakra Clearing, Intuitive Readings, and personalized spiritual healing sessions. My approach is intuitive and individualized, recognizing that each person is different and may benefit from a different type of support. I draw from more than a decade of energy work along with my background as a Certified Biofeedback Specialist, Reconnective Healing® Foundational Practitioner, Brain Gym® Consultant, Touch for Health® practitioner, and Certified Educator. I also offer My Healing Sanctuary, a private healing retreat on our wooded property in Priest River, Idaho, for those who would like to step away from everyday life, enjoy quiet personal time, and receive overnight Quantum Biofeedback sessions.
