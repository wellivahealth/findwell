About the practice

I hold a degree in Health Sciences from James Madison University and am currently earning my Master of Arts in Counseling at Eastern Mennonite University, and I bring that training to every coaching relationship.

I work alongside my clients, not over them. My style is direct: I ask a lot of questions and set goals we can actually measure, so progress is clear week to week. Whether you’re a student learning to manage your time, a parent supporting a teen, or an adult navigating a health condition or a big life change, we’ll build systems that fit your life and that you can own.

My own journey through significant chronic health challenges deeply shaped my understanding of healing, perseverance, and personal growth. It taught me that wellness is rarely a perfect or linear process. Often, meaningful breakthrough begins with small, intentional steps taken with courage, compassion, and grace.

At On Track Wellness Coaching, I strive to create a supportive space where clients feel heard, encouraged, and empowered as they navigate life’s challenges and pursue lasting, meaningful change.

I believe meaningful change can begin with a single conversation. I specialize in coaching teens, college students, and adults with ADHD and executive function challenges. I also support clients who are:

Managing a chronic health or neurological condition

Navigating pregnancy or new parenthood

Going through a midlife transition

Feeling stressed, stuck, or overwhelmed
