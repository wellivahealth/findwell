About the practice

For women who have spent years holding everyone else, Desiree Maultsby, MTP, offers a place to finally slow down. The nervous system regulation work blends qi gong, breathwork, guided visualization, and process art, grounded in more than two decades of somatic and expressive arts practice. Through Sacred Circle Institute, she leads women’s retreats, creative circles, and private sessions in Tucson and online.

I’m Desiree Maultsby, a Brooklyn-born, Tucson-based facilitator with more than twenty years in somatic practice, ceremonial work, and expressive arts. Through Sacred Circle Institute, I help women come home to their bodies and reclaim their creative wisdom. My sessions weave qi gong, breathwork, guided visualization, and process art (painting, mixed media, writing, and movement) into a practice of presence rather than performance. No art experience is needed. My work follows the PARC Process: Land, Listen, Make, Honor, Return. Together we slow down, settle the body, follow what wants to be made, and carry its wisdom back into daily life. I especially love working with women who have spent years holding everyone else and are ready to claim their own voice. Offerings include women’s retreats, private sessions, monthly online painting circles, group programs, and facilitation for organizations and communities.
