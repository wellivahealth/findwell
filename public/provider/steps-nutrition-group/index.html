About the practice

Miriam Wiener, MS, MPH, founded S.T.E.P.S. Nutrition Group to provide quality, evidence-based nutrition therapy for women and girls. A licensed dietitian-nutritionist and board-certified nutrition specialist with over 30 years of experience, she is known for her warm approach and sympathetic ear, combined with highly effective tools and techniques that work.

Miriam Wiener, MS, MPH, founded S.T.E.P.S. Nutrition Group to provide quality, evidence-based nutrition therapy for women and girls. A licensed dietitian-nutritionist and board-certified nutrition specialist with over 30 years of experience, she is known for her warm approach and sympathetic ear, combined with highly effective tools and techniques that work. Miriam is a graduate of UCLA and the UNC Gillings School of Global Public Health, where she developed an award-winning diabetes prevention and management program based on her trademarked S.T.E.P.S. to Nourishment® framework. She is a grant reviewer for the IHS Department of Health and Human Services Division of Diabetes Treatment and Prevention, and the founder and executive director of Nourishment of the Soul, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to bringing foundational, evidence-based nutrition education to women and girls. Miriam has been featured in the Jewish Journal of Greater Los Angeles.
