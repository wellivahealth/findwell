About the practice

At The Whole Story, I help older adults strengthen their bones and improve bone mineral density using nutrition and lifestyle. Clients can choose from 1:1 or group sessions. When you work with me, you will experience a food-first approach, gentle nutrition and compassionate care.

I help older adults to improve their bone density and strengthen their bones. In my practice I find that people often feel fragile and overwhelmed after being told they’re at high risk for fractures. Many people tell me that they feel like it is a full time job just to eat the right foods and take the right supplements to strengthen their bones. Together we work on simplifying the process and building strength, resilience and confidence in their body. I am a Certified Nutrition Specialist based in Virginia, and licensed in Maryland, Pennsylvania, North Carolina and Illinois. I have worked in the field of Nutrition for the past 40 years. When you work with me, you will experience a food-first approach, gentle nutrition and compassionate care.
