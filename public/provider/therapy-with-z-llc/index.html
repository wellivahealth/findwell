About the practice

Therapy with Z, LLC provides inclusive, person-centered online psychotherapy for adolescents (13+), adults, and older adults. We specialize in anxiety, depression, trauma, chronic illness and pain, grief, and LGBTQIA+ affirming care, offering compassionate, evidence-based support tailored to each client's unique needs and goals. Telehealth services are available in Oklahoma, Florida, South Carolina, and Idaho.

Therapy with Z, LLC is an inclusive, person-centered online psychotherapy practice serving adolescents (13+), adults, and older adults. We specialize in anxiety, depression, trauma and PTSD, chronic illness and pain, grief and loss, stress and burnout, and LGBTQIA+ affirming care. Our integrative, trauma-informed approach combines evidence-based psychotherapy, mindfulness, self-compassion, and practical coping skills to support emotional well-being and personal growth. Telehealth services are available in Oklahoma, Florida, South Carolina, and Idaho.

Fees

Individual psychotherapy: $75 for 30-minute sessions or $150 for 60-minute sessions. Insurance is accepted through participating networks, with copays, coinsurance, and deductibles varying by plan. A complimentary 15-minute consultation is available.

Payment: Insurance, Cash, Credit Cards. Insurance: Accepted — verify your plan with the practice.
