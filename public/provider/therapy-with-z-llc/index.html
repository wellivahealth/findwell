Therapy with Z, LLC is an inclusive, person-centered online psychotherapy practice serving adolescents (13+), adults, and older adults. We specialize in anxiety, depression, trauma and PTSD, chronic illness and pain, grief and loss, stress and burnout, and LGBTQIA+ affirming care. Our integrative, trauma-informed approach combines evidence-based psychotherapy, mindfulness, self-compassion, and practical coping skills to support emotional well-being and personal growth. Telehealth services are available in Oklahoma, Florida, South Carolina, and Idaho.
