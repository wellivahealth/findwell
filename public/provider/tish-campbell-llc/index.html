About the practice

My name is Tish Campbell, and I am very excited for the opportunity to help guide you on your healing—or health optimizing—journey! I am a long-time health and wellness enthusiast, researcher, and educator. I believe this unique combination provides an enormous benefit for my clients.

I whole-heartedly believe in the motto “You can’t change what you can’t measure” and I lean heavily on behavior change theory and S.M.A.R.T. goals to help my clients improve their health and their lives! I know that this is a bold claim, but I'm confident that by working with me you will feel better, be healthier, and thus happier. :-)
