Wholistic Medical Group is a Tampa-based integrative healthcare practice founded by Dr. Duke Mansell, DOM, AP, in 2016. We combine functional medicine, acupuncture, functional nutrition, dry needling and natural therapies with a systems-based approach to health. We work with patients experiencing persistent concerns including digestive issues, chronic fatigue and low energy, chronic pain and inflammation, hormone health, headaches and migraines. Rather than focusing only on individual symptoms, our approach considers contributing factors across the body and develops individualized strategies designed to support better function and long-term health. In-person care is available in Tampa, with telehealth options also available.
