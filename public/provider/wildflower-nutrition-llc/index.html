About the practice

Wildflower Nutrition is a functional nutrition practice helping health-conscious women navigate fatigue, digestive concerns, hormonal changes, and the overwhelm of conflicting nutrition advice. Through personalized nutrition, lab interpretation, and health coaching, I help women better understand their bodies, identify what’s contributing to their symptoms, and build sustainable habits that support their health. My approach combines evidence-based nutrition with practical behavior change to help women feel more energized, confident in their choices, and empowered to live the lives they want.

Wildflower Nutrition is a functional nutrition and health coaching practice founded by Stephanie Hands, MS, CNS, NBC-HWC. I specialize in helping women navigate fatigue, digestive issues, hormonal changes, metabolic health concerns, and the overwhelm that often comes with trying to improve their health. My approach combines evidence-based nutrition, functional lab assessment, and personalized health coaching to understand the underlying factors contributing to symptoms. I consider the whole person, including nutrition, lifestyle, health history, stress, and individual goals, to create practical recommendations that fit into everyday life. Whether you’re struggling with persistent symptoms, feeling confused by conflicting health advice, or simply want to feel better in your body, Wildflower Nutrition provides individualized support to help you make informed decisions, build confidence in your choices, and develop sustainable habits. My mission is to help women feel more energized, trust their bodies, and have more energy for the lives they want to live.

Fees

Wildflower Nutrition offers several options depending on your individual needs and goals:
1. Comprehensive Nutrition Consultation: $350 for a 90-minute consultation, including a thorough review of health history, nutrition, labs, and a personalized written action plan.
2. Root to Restore™ 1:1 Nutrition & Health Coaching: A 4-month personalized program including an initial consultation, seven follow-up sessions, lab interpretation, individualized nutrition recommendations, and ongoing support. Payment plans available starting at $284/month.
3. Additional Nutrition Support Packages: Available based on individual needs. Contact us to explore options.
Payment: Cash, Credit Cards, Checks, Debit Cards, HSA/FSA. Insurance: Not accepted.
