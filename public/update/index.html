Update your listing

Practitioners can change their own listing. We'll email a secure link to the address on the listing, so only you can open it.

Listing: Choose your practice

What happens next

The link opens a form with your listing already filled in. Change what you need and press save. Most changes are live within a couple of minutes.

A few changes are checked by us first: adding a discipline, and changes to your license or credentials, which we confirm with the board again before your listing shows them as confirmed. Changing the email address on your listing needs a click from the new address.

No longer have access to the email on your listing? Write to us at info@findwelldirectory.com.
