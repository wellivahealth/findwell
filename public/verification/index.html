What verification means here

Every listing says plainly how its credentials were established. There are two states, and neither is a badge.

As reported by the practitioner. Not independently verified.

The practitioner told us their credentials and we published what they said. We have not checked it with anyone. This is a normal, permanent state for a listing here — not a queue we are working through.

License AC-4821 confirmed with the Arizona Acupuncture Board of Examiners, 25 Aug 2026.

We checked directly with the body that issued the credential, and we name that body and the date we checked. A credential can lapse after a check, which is why the date is there.

Three things people confuse

  • A state license is issued by a government board that can revoke it. Naturopathic physicians, acupuncturists, chiropractors, counselors and massage therapists are licensed in Arizona. A license number can be checked against the issuing board by anyone, including you.
  • A certification comes from a private body with its own training requirements and assessment — NAMA board certification in Ayurveda, NBC-HWC for health coaches. Real, and meaningful, but no government stands behind it and nobody can be struck off a public register.
  • A membership is something you join, usually by paying. It is not a credential, and we do not treat it as one, whatever it is called.

Disciplines with no license at all

Ayurveda, herbalism, energy work and end-of-life support have no state licensure anywhere in the United States. That is not a gap in our checking — there is no register to check. For those listings we publish the training programme, the hours and any voluntary certification, and we say that no licensure exists.

What practitioners agree to

Everyone who applies confirms that the credentials they give us are accurate and current, and agrees to tell us if anything changes, lapses or is suspended. If you believe a listing is inaccurate, tell us and we will look into it.

What a listing is not

A listing is not a referral, a recommendation, or a judgement about anyone's competence. We publish credentials as stated and, where we have checked them, we say who with and when. Choosing a practitioner remains yours to do.
