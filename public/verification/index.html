How we verify

Every listing says plainly how its credentials were established. There are two states, and you will see one of them on every listing.

Confirmed with the Arizona Acupuncture Board of Examiners

License AC-4821, checked 25 Aug 2026.

We checked directly with the body that issued the credential, and we name that body and the date we checked. A credential can lapse after a check, which is why the date is there.

As reported by the practitioner

We publish what the practitioner told us, and say so.

The practitioner told us their credentials and we published what they said. This is a normal, lasting state for a listing here, not a queue we are working through.

Three things people confuse

A state license

Issued by a government board that can revoke it. Acupuncturists, chiropractors, naturopathic doctors, counselors and massage therapists are licensed. Anyone can check a license number with the board, including you.

A certification

Comes from a private body with its own training and assessment, such as NAMA board certification in Ayurveda or NBC-HWC for health coaches. Real and meaningful, though no government stands behind it.

A membership

Something you join, usually by paying. It is not a credential, and we do not treat it as one, whatever it is called.

Disciplines with no license at all

Ayurveda, herbalism, energy work and end-of-life support have no state licensure anywhere in the United States. That is not a gap in our checking, because there is no register to check. For those listings we publish the training, the hours and any certification, and we say that no license exists.

What practitioners agree to

Everyone who applies confirms that the credentials they give us are accurate and current, and agrees to tell us if anything changes, lapses or is suspended. If you believe a listing is inaccurate, tell us and we will look into it.

What a listing is not

A listing is not a referral, a recommendation, or a judgement about anyone's competence. We publish credentials as stated and, where we have checked them, we say who with and when. Choosing a practitioner remains yours to do.
